## Priority Issues

**[P1] The hero omits the two numbers PRODUCT.md calls the first-scan credibility bar.** *(reclassified from the sub-agent's P0 — this doesn't block the task, since scrolling still surfaces the numbers, but it does directly undercut a named product principle for anyone who doesn't scroll)*
- **Why it matters**: PRODUCT.md's Product Principle #3 states "Optimize for a hiring manager's first-scan credibility: scope (1,000+ apps, ~8 years)..." Neither number appears in the hero (name, tagline, email, title, certs only) — both live only in the About section's stat-list, past a full scroll and a headline paragraph.
- **Fix**: Add a compact stat strip under the hero tagline (reuse the existing stat-list numeral treatment at a smaller scale), or fold the two numbers into the `.meta-row`.
- **Suggested command**: $impeccable clarify

**[P1] The one true differentiator — continuity across three roles — is asserted in text but not shown structurally.**
- **Why it matters**: PRODUCT.md frames this continuity as the mechanism a neighboring candidate couldn't truthfully copy, but the three Renault `.exp-role` blocks sit inside one `.exp-body` with no visual connector distinguishing them from three unrelated employers stacked as separate rows below. A skimmer sees "four companies," not "one throughline plus three prior roles."
- **Fix**: Add a slim vertical spine/connector linking the three role blocks inside the Renault row, and/or a small "current" marker on the ongoing role, visually distinct from how the collapsed peer companies render.
- **Suggested command**: $impeccable layout

**[P1] `ink-faint`/`ivory-faint` still fail WCAG AA — flagged in the first critique, never fixed.** *(verified independently: `rgba(27,27,25,0.42)` on `#E4E3DC` = 2.52:1; `rgba(243,240,230,0.44)` on `#20211B` = 3.87:1 — both need 4.5:1; `ink-dim`/`ivory-dim` are fine at 4.68:1 / 7.27:1)*
- **Why it matters**: This is the same class of problem as the gold-contrast fix from last pass, on the tier below it — `.section-summary`, `.exp-when` role dates, `.stat-list .lbl`, and the contact row labels ("Email"/"Phone") all use the failing `-faint` tier for real, load-bearing information, not decoration.
- **Fix**: Raise the alpha/lightness of `ink-faint` and `ivory-faint` until both clear 4.5:1 at their smallest use sizes, following the same measured approach already applied to `--gold`.
- **Suggested command**: $impeccable harden

**[P2] `.skill-cat` has no responsive collapse breakpoint, unlike every other two-column moment in the system.**
- **Why it matters**: `.about-grid` collapses at 820px and `.exp-role` at 640px, but `.skill-cat`'s `.cat-name{width:190px}` never stacks. On a ~390px viewport, roughly 136px is left for the 16-tag Google Cloud category after the fixed label and gap — inconsistent with how the system was designed to behave everywhere else.
- **Fix**: `@media (max-width:640px){ .skill-cat{flex-direction:column;} .skill-cat .cat-name{width:auto;} }`.
- **Suggested command**: $impeccable layout
